## Why a whole lesson on vocabulary

Walk into almost any IT support role and you'll hear a wall of acronyms on
day one, used as if everyone already knows them. This lesson is a fast tour
of the terms that come up most — the full reference lives in the
[glossary](/glossary) if you ever need to look one up again later.

## Identity and access

[SSO](/glossary/sso) (Single Sign-On) lets someone log in once and reach
every system they're authorized for, instead of juggling a dozen separate
passwords. The system that holds those identities — who exists, what
group they're in, what they're allowed to touch — is often
[AD](/glossary/ad) (Active Directory) on a Windows-based network. AD applies
rules to users and computers through [GPO](/glossary/gpo)s (Group Policy
Objects): a GPO might enforce a minimum password length, deploy software, or
lock a desktop background across an entire department at once.

## Managing devices

[MDM](/glossary/mdm) (Mobile Device Management) is the device-side
counterpart to AD — it enrolls, configures, secures, and can remotely wipe
laptops and phones. Every device MDM manages is an
[endpoint](/glossary/endpoint): company terminology for "a thing that
connects to the network," whether that's a laptop, a phone, or a server.

## Support and service terms

When someone reports a problem, it becomes a ticket in a
[ticket queue](/glossary/ticket-queue) — the ordered backlog of open issues
waiting to be worked. If the first person who picks it up can't resolve it,
they perform an [escalation](/glossary/escalation): handing it to a more
senior or specialized team rather than letting it sit unresolved. How fast a
ticket is supposed to be answered and fixed is usually governed by an
[SLA](/glossary/sla) (Service Level Agreement) — a commitment the support
team has made about response and resolution time.

## Putting it together

None of these terms are complicated on their own — the hard part is just
that they all get thrown around at once, in conversation, without
definition. Once you've got SSO, AD, GPO, MDM, endpoint, ticket queue,
escalation, and SLA solid, most day-one conversations in an IT support role
stop sounding like a different language.
